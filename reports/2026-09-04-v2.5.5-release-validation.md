## State-machine recording

The TrackMap timed recording captured 300/300 frames at 60 FPS for exactly
5.0 seconds. It decoded completely with zero repeated frames, no warnings, and
216 distinct visual frames. Five scheduled enum, number, pointer-down, and
pointer-up actions were applied at frames 30, 60, 90, 93, and 150 with zero
lateness. Maximum observed capture lag was 43.33 ms; it did not change frame
count, duration, or interaction timing.

A separate manual recording accepted live ViewModel and pointer operations,
then stopped through MCP. It captured, received, and decoded 99/99 frames at
60 FPS over 1.65 seconds, with zero repeated frames and no warnings. A 30-second
APNG recording cancelled promptly and did not publish its destination.

There is no product duration, frame-count, job-lifetime, output-size, or spool
ceiling. Capture remains bounded by available disk, one active job, the
documented pixel/FPS limits, memory/stall guards, and the 128 MiB disk reserve.
